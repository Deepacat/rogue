KubeJS data & assets folders will almost ALWAYS take priority over other methods of adding data/assets

This means that anything in this folder 99% of the type will be un-overrideable by another resource pack

However it can be nice to allow your custom assets to be override-able, 
    so users may make their own resourcepacks for your custom modpack assets

In the case that you do want users to be able to create resourcepacks and override your assets,
    use the `/mineraft/config/paxi/resourcepacks/custom_pack_assets/assets/` folder which is
    a force enabled resourcepack with editable priority for users
